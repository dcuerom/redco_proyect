## 1. Hatch  (reemplaza a SRK — SRK sin declaración nítida publicada)

**Propósito (Manifesto oficial, hatch.com):**
> "Passionately committed to the pursuit of a better world through positive change."

**Ethos / lema:**
> "Entrepreneurs with a technical soul."

**Sobre el Manifesto:** Hatch declara que su Manifesto ES su ethos, credo y su
misión/visión/valores; "guides who we are as a company and why we exist."

Employee-owned e independiente. Fundada en Toronto (1955). ~11.000 profesionales;
sectores: metales/minería, energía e infraestructura. Seis compromisos: diversidad e
inclusión, sostenibilidad, salud y seguridad, calidad, ética e integridad, y comunidad.

Fuentes:
- https://www.hatch.com/About-Us/About-the-Company
- https://www.hatch.com/About-Us/About-the-Company/Our-Manifesto

## 2. McKinsey & Company

**Propósito (mckinsey.com/about-us/overview/our-purpose-mission-and-values):**
> "To help create positive, enduring change in the world."

**Misión (dos componentes; herencia de James O. McKinsey y Marvin Bower):**

> performance, and to build a great firm that attracts, develops, excites, and retains
> exceptional people."

## 3. WSP (matriz que absorbió a Golder Associates en 2021)

**Propósito (wsp.com):**
> "We exist to shape communities to advance humanity."

**Misión / visión (variantes documentadas):**
> "To be the premier professional services firm, delivering sustainable solutions for our
> clients, communities, and the environment."
> Visión: "To be the world's leading engineering and professional services firm... always
> the first choice for clients, partners, and employees."

**Enfoque distintivo — Future Ready®:**
> "We see the world through a Future Ready® lens, fuelled by a shared commitment to making
> a positive impact — one project and community at a time."

Valores centrales: integrity, innovation, collaboration, sustainability, safety, inclusion.

Fuentes:
- https://www.wsp.com/en-us/who-we-are
